Our Home Delivery Service

Stellah's Bakery provides home delivery services to customers who are unable to visit our bakery.

How to Order

  1. Choose the products you want.
  2. Contact us by phone or email.
  3. Give us your delivery location.
  4. Confirm your order.
  5. Receive your products at your home.

Delivery charges depend on the distance and size of the order.
